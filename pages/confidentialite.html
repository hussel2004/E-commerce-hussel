Our Privacy Commitment

We respect your privacy. Below is a concise summary of what we collect and why.

1. Data Collection

We collect only the information necessary to process orders and deliver products: name, email, delivery address and payment details (handled securely by payment providers).

2. Use of Data

Your data is used to fulfil orders, provide customer support, and improve our services. Marketing messages are sent only if you opt in; you can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Security

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect personal data and work with trusted providers who are contractually bound to safeguard your information.

4. Your Rights

You can access, correct, delete or export your data. For requests, contact our privacy team using the contact details on the right.
